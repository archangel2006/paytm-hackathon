# Paytm Saathi Fee Policy

**MOCK DATA — Not real Paytm pricing. For demo purposes only.**

## Soundbox Rental

The Paytm Soundbox is available on a monthly rental plan.

- Monthly rental: ₹125 per month (including GST)
- First month is free for new merchants
- Rental is deducted from daily settlements
- No upfront payment required

## UPI Transaction Charges

- UPI payments below ₹2,000: No charge (0%)
- UPI payments above ₹2,000: 0.25% of transaction value
- Maximum charge per transaction: ₹25
- Charges are settled monthly

## Card Payments

- Debit card (RuPay): 0% MDR
- Debit card (Visa/Mastercard): 0.4% MDR
- Credit card: 1.5% MDR

## Settlement Timeline

- Payments received before 5 PM settle the next working day (T+1)
- Payments received after 5 PM settle in 2 working days (T+2)
- Settlements are credited directly to the registered bank account

## Bank Holiday Delays

If the settlement date falls on a bank holiday or weekend, settlement is deferred to the next working day.

## Refund Policy

Refunds are processed within 5–7 working days. Contact support for refund requests.

## Device Security Deposit

No security deposit is required for the Soundbox.

## Contact for Fee Queries

For any questions about charges or billing, contact Paytm merchant support.
