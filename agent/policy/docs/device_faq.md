# Paytm Soundbox Device FAQ

**MOCK DATA — For demo purposes only.**

## Getting Started

### How do I set up my Soundbox?
1. Power on the Soundbox by pressing the power button
2. Connect to the internet using the built-in SIM card or Wi-Fi
3. Open the Paytm for Business app and follow the setup instructions
4. Ask your first customer to scan the QR code to make a payment

### How does the Soundbox announce payments?
When a customer pays via UPI, the Soundbox announces the amount in your chosen language (Hindi or English). The announcement happens within 2–3 seconds of the payment.

## Troubleshooting

### Soundbox not announcing payments
1. Check if the volume is turned up (press the volume+ button)
2. Ensure the device is connected to the internet
3. Restart the device by holding the power button for 5 seconds
4. Check if the Paytm for Business app is active on your phone

### No internet connection
1. Check if the SIM card is properly inserted
2. Ensure you are in an area with network coverage
3. Try toggling the device off and on
4. Contact support if the problem persists

### QR code not scanning
1. Ensure the QR code sticker is clean and unobstructed
2. The QR code should be printed — a phone screen may not scan well
3. Request a new QR sticker from your field executive

### Payment received but Soundbox did not announce
1. Check the internet connection
2. Verify the amount in the Paytm for Business app
3. The announcement delay can be 5–10 seconds in low connectivity areas

## Battery and Power

- Battery life: up to 24 hours on a single charge
- Charging time: approximately 2 hours
- Use the provided USB cable and charger only

## Maintenance

- Clean the device with a dry cloth — do not use liquids
- Keep the device away from direct sunlight and water
- Do not disassemble the device

## Support

For device issues not resolved by the above steps, contact Paytm merchant support or ask your field executive during their next visit.
