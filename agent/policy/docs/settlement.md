# Paytm Saathi Settlement Policy

**MOCK DATA — Not real Paytm data. For demo purposes only.**

## How Settlements Work

Paytm settles merchant payments directly to the registered bank account.

## Settlement Schedule

- Standard settlement: T+1 (next working day)
- Payments received before 5:00 PM: settled next working day
- Payments received after 5:00 PM: settled in 2 working days
- Working days: Monday to Friday, excluding bank holidays

## Checking Settlement Status

Merchants can check settlement status in the Paytm for Business app or by contacting Paytm Saathi.

## Bank Holiday Delay

When the settlement date falls on a bank holiday or national holiday, the amount is credited on the next working day.

Example: A payment on Friday evening may settle on Monday (or Tuesday if Monday is a holiday).

## Delay Reasons

| Reason | Typical Resolution |
|---|---|
| Bank holiday | Next working day |
| Bank technical error | 24–48 hours after the bank resolves it |
| Pending KYC verification | After KYC is approved |
| Bank account mismatch | Contact support to update account details |

## Minimum Settlement Amount

- Settlements are processed when the accumulated amount is ₹1 or more
- Amounts below ₹1 are carried forward to the next settlement cycle

## Failed Settlement

If a settlement fails (for example due to a wrong account number), the amount is held and a support ticket is created. The merchant will be contacted within 24 hours.

## Tax Deduction at Source (TDS)

TDS is deducted as per Income Tax rules for merchants with annual payments exceeding the applicable threshold.
