# Paytm Saathi KYC Requirements

**MOCK DATA — For demo purposes only. This is a pre-check only, NOT official KYC verification.**

## Required Documents

To activate a Paytm merchant account, the following documents are required:

### 1. PAN Card (Mandatory)
- A clear photo of the PAN card
- Must show the full PAN number (5 letters, 4 digits, 1 letter — e.g. ABCDE1234F)
- Name on the PAN must match the registered business owner name
- Photo must be sharp, fully visible, and unobstructed

### 2. Address Proof (Mandatory)
Acceptable address proofs:
- Aadhaar Card
- Electricity Bill (not older than 3 months)
- Bank Statement (not older than 3 months)
- Voter ID Card
- Passport

## Document Quality Requirements

- Image must be in focus (not blurry)
- Full document must be visible — no cropping of corners or text
- Good lighting — text must be readable
- File size: maximum 10 MB
- Accepted formats: JPG, PNG, PDF

## Pre-Check vs. Official KYC

The Saathi document pre-check verifies:
- Image quality (sharpness)
- PAN format validity
- Name match with the registered profile

**This pre-check is NOT the official KYC verification.** The official KYC decision is made by the Paytm KYC system. The pre-check helps catch common issues early so the merchant does not have to resubmit.

## Common Rejection Reasons

| Issue | What to Do |
|---|---|
| Photo is blurry | Retake the photo in good lighting, keep hands steady |
| PAN number not readable | Ensure the card is flat and fully visible |
| Name does not match | Verify the name matches your registered profile |
| Document is cropped | Include the full document in the frame |
