# Paytm Merchant Onboarding & KYC Documentation Policy

## Overview
This document specifies the mandatory documentation, quality standards, and verification guidelines required before a merchant can receive a Paytm Soundbox or POS device.

## 1. Required KYC Documents
Every merchant must provide two valid proofs:
1. **Permanent Account Number (PAN) Card:**
   - Individual PAN for Sole Proprietorship.
   - Company / Partnership PAN for registered legal entities.
   - Name on the PAN card must match the registered merchant profile and bank account name.
2. **Business Proof or Address Proof:**
   - Aadhaar Card (front and back).
   - Electricity / Utility bill (issued within the last 3 months).
   - Shop & Establishment Certificate, GSTIN Certificate, or Udyam Aadhar.

## 2. Document Quality and Pre-Check Guidelines
- **Sharpness & Focus:** Text, numbers, and the photograph must be sharp and legible. Blurry images with a Laplacian variance score below threshold are rejected.
- **Framing:** All 4 corners of the identity card must be clearly visible. No cropped edges.
- **Lighting & Glare:** No flash reflections, shadows, or thumb/finger obscuring names or PAN numbers.
- **Targeted Re-Request:** If a photo fails the pre-check, the agent must reject only the unreadable document and ask politely for a clear photo of that specific document.

## 3. Regulatory Disclaimer (Important)
- **Pre-Check Scope:** The automated blur and OCR pre-check performed by Paytm Saathi is strictly a quality check to assist the merchant.
- **Verification Authority:** Final KYC approval is governed exclusively by the official Paytm KYC verification engine and compliance officers. The AI teammate never states that a document is "verified by official KYC".
