# Paytm Soundbox: Fees, Rental & Pricing Policy

## Overview
This document specifies the official pricing, rental rates, and payment processing charges for Paytm Soundbox and POS devices. All representatives and AI teammates must quote strictly from these figures.

## 1. Soundbox Monthly Rental
- **Standard Soundbox (4G Voice Box):** ₹125 per month (+ GST).
- **Billing Cycle:** Rental is automatically deducted from monthly settlements in the first week of every calendar month.
- **Security Deposit:** One-time refundable security deposit of ₹299 paid at installation. Refundable upon device return in good working condition.

## 2. Transaction Charges (MDR)
- **UPI Payments (all apps - Paytm, PhonePe, Google Pay, BHIM):** **0% MDR (Zero Transaction Fee)**.
- **RuPay Debit Cards:** **0% MDR**.
- **Visa / Mastercard Debit Cards:** 0.40% for transaction amounts up to ₹2,000; 0.90% for amounts above ₹2,000.
- **Credit Cards (Visa / Mastercard / RuPay):** 1.99% (+ GST).
- **Paytm Postpaid / Wallet:** 1.50% (+ GST).

## 3. Fee Waivers and Discount Policy
- **Autonomous Waivers:** AI teammates and Field Sales Executives (FSEs) are **strictly prohibited** from offering discounts, fee waivers, or free rentals autonomously.
- **Promotional Waivers:** Waivers require **Level 2 (L2) Sales Manager approval**.
- **Volume-linked Rebate:** Merchants processing over 500 UPI transactions per month qualify for a ₹25/month rental cashback, subject to manager sign-off.
