# Paytm Soundbox: Hardware, Setup & Troubleshooting FAQ

## Overview
Technical guidelines and operational instructions for Paytm Soundbox 4G audio confirmation devices.

## 1. Installation & Optimal Placement
- **Counter Placement:** Place the Soundbox on the main billing counter facing customer footfall, approximately 1 to 2 feet away from payment QR stands.
- **Environment:** Keep away from high moisture, grease, direct sunlight, and heat sources.

## 2. Audio & Language Settings
- **Voice Confirmation:** Instantly announces received payments in real-time (e.g., "Paytm par pachaas rupaye prapt hue").
- **Supported Languages:** Hindi, English, Hinglish, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Punjabi, and Malayalam.
- **Volume Adjustment:** Use the `+` and `-` physical buttons on the device side. To change announcement language, hold both volume buttons for 3 seconds.

## 3. Connectivity & SIM
- **Built-in 4G SIM:** The Soundbox comes with a pre-configured multi-network eSIM that automatically switches between Airtel, Jio, and Vi networks based on signal strength.
- **Wi-Fi Connectivity:** Wi-Fi can be paired as an auxiliary backup through the Paytm for Business app.

## 4. Battery & Power
- **Battery Life:** Internal rechargeable lithium-ion battery provides up to 7 to 10 days of continuous operation.
- **Charging:** Recharge using the included Type-C USB cable and 5V power adapter. A full charge takes approximately 2 hours.

## 5. LED Indicator Diagnostic Codes
- **Solid Blue Light:** Device is online, connected to network, and ready to accept payments.
- **Flashing Blue Light:** Processing payment audio alert.
- **Flashing Red Light:** Network searching or weak cellular signal.
- **Solid Red Light:** Battery level is critically low (under 15%). Plug in charger.
